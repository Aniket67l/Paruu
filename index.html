Dear Pari, 💌

Happy birthday, meri pyaari Paruu. 🎂💗

I don't think you realise how many little things about you make you special to me. Your voice notes, your random stories, your logic, your caring side, your cute expressions, your nakhre—and the way you can go from the softest Paruu to the most intimidating baddie in two seconds. 😭

I still remember how unexpectedly our conversations started. A group chat, a little argument, one compliment, and then a DM that turned into so many talks and calls. And that rooftop conversation… “mai hoon na, mai seekha dunga.” I didn't know then how important those ordinary little moments would become.

I admire how smart and capable you are, but I also hope you remember that you don't have to be perfect or strong every second. You deserve kindness, respect, laughter, and people who listen when you speak. You can be silly, quiet, confident, emotional, or all of them in one day—you still get to be yourself.

This little website isn't a grand gift. It's just my way of collecting a few memories and saying: I'm glad I got to know you. I hope this new year of your life brings you reasons to smile, things to be proud of, and people who make you feel safe and appreciated.

Keep being your wonderfully unpredictable self, Madam. And please don't use your baddie powers to bully the website-maker too much. 😭🖤

Happy Birthday, Pari. ♡
— your resident bandar, Aniket 🐒
